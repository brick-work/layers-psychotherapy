About

Jack Hambrick, LMFT

People come to therapy because they want something to change. We can start there.

I'm Jack Hambrick, a licensed marriage and family therapist offering online therapy throughout Ohio. I work with adolescents, adults, couples, and families, specializing in ADHD, anxiety, depression, sexuality, and identity.

My approach to therapy is conversational and collaborative. I ask questions; that's my thing. I listen. I help you understand what's keeping you stuck, and I work with you to get closer to what you want.

With couples, I focus on the underlying patterns of unspoken expectations, of power, and of disconnection. Together we'll build a relationship that works for both of you.

Sessions are available afternoons and evenings on a $35 to $75 sliding scale. Reach out when you're ready.
